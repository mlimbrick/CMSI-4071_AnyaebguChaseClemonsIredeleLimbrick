INTRODUCTION SECTION:
   System objectives section
      Strengths
         Objectives are understandable and user focused.
         Good discussion of marketplace trust.
         Architectural maintainability is considered.
         Deployment portability is considered.
      Suggestions
         Objectives should explicitly address booking workflows,
            notifications, moderation, and reviews because they are
            required by the SRS.
         The security objective should include account verification rather
            than solely .edu format validation.
         Administrative objectives should be represented.

   Hardware, software, and human interfaces section
      Strengths
         Very complete addressing of all major listed components.
         Complete treatment of user-facing interfaces.
         Hardware interfaces are clearly identified.
         Software dependencies are documented.
         Human-interface descriptions are detailed.
         Interface numbering is consistent.
      Suggestions
         Include cloud services specified in the requirements.
         Include identity service interfaces.
         Include notification-service interfaces.
         Include object-storage interfaces.
         Include monitoring and operational-service interfaces.

COMMENTS:
The architectural SDD is well written, easy to follow, and provides a
coherent layered architecture.  The document demonstrates good architectural
thinking through separation of concerns, clear component responsibilities,
explicit interface descriptions, and appropriate architectural diagrams.
However, there are significant consistency gaps between the SRS and the SDD.
The SRS defines a substantially larger system that includes booking,
reviews, notifications, moderation, administrator functions, email
verification, platform services, cloud deployment, object storage, and
operational infrastructure.  The SDD narrows the scope considerably and
documents a simpler prototype architecture.  As a result, many required
CSCs, functions, and performance goals are not represented architecturally.

The document demonstrates solid architectural skills, clear writing, good
decomposition, and useful diagrams.  The architecture itself is reasonable
for the functionality it includes.  The largest issue is scope mismatch.
The SRS defines a much larger system, but Tte SDD documents only a subset
of the SRS capabilities.

Overall Rating: Adequate Architectural Design for a Prototype, but not yet
adequate for the Full Required System
